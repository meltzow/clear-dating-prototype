# CLEAR Dating Prototype

Focused product concept test for CLEAR as a dating marketplace.

## Current test: v2.3 marketplace

CLEAR remains a dating app / marketplace. The reduced test isolates one differentiating product mechanism inside that marketplace:

> After a CLEAR match leads to an unclear dating situation, does the Let-Them/Let-Me-style support help people become clearer about what they themselves want and what they want to do next?

The business-level hypothesis is:

> Is that support valuable enough to make CLEAR more attractive as a dating app and give users a reason to try it compared with dating apps that do not offer this kind of guidance?

Secondary signal: does the situation occupy the user less mentally after the CLEAR flow?

## 10-screen flow

1. Dating preference for the example (women / men / either) and CLEAR established as the dating app
2. One unclear situation after a CLEAR match and first date
3. BEFORE: clarity + mental load
4. CLEAR separates what is known from what is still open
5. The other person's part
6. The user's own needs
7. The user's own next action
8. AFTER: same clarity + mental-load questions
9. Marketplace value: is this useful inside a dating app, and is it a reason to try CLEAR?
10. Open feedback, including what CLEAR would need to do differently from existing dating apps

Not part of this test: profile browsing, swipe mechanics, matching quality, Reality Log, AI Coach, pricing, or Mel Robbins branding.
